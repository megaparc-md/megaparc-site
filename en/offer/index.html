01What we buy

Four kinds of property.

All our current properties are in the Republic of Moldova. We also consider offers from other countries — each on its own merits.

  1. 01

    Operating commercial buildings

    Retail, offices, services — buildings that already work or could work better.

  2. 02

    Buildings to renovate or reposition

    Old or empty buildings in a good location that deserve a new use.

  3. 03

    Land for development

    Plots in town or at its entrance, with access and clear potential.

  4. 04

    Projects already started

    Projects with permits or construction under way that need a new owner.

What information is useful?

  • Location
  • Cadastral number
  • Land and building area
  • Photographs
  • Ownership
  • Current use
  • Asking price, if available
  • Planning documents
  • Utilities
  • Occupancy and leases

Not everything is needed at once. The more initial data, the faster we can tell whether it makes sense to continue the discussion.
