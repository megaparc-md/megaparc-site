How we work

Four owner's rules.

  1. We buy what we understand

    Location, building, market and risks — before price.

  2. Function before form

    First who will use the space and how, then the architecture.

  3. We build to operate

    Our buildings stay with us, so quality shows in tomorrow's costs.

  4. The tenant must succeed

    A good space is one where the tenant's business works.

We run our own buildings ourselves.

Leasing, tenant relationships, maintenance and investment in improvements are handled by the MEGAPARC team — for MEGAPARC buildings only. We do not take other owners' buildings under management.

Behind every building is our own team: the Technical Director, the Architect, leasing and operations.
