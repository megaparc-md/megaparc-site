Drochia Gateway

A 2.0 ha site in Drochia, under consideration for commercial, logistics or mixed-use development.

01Concept

A development site at the entrance to the town.

The site at Bd. Independenței 65 (20,000 m²) has two road fronts and is clearly visible from the road into the town. Separate access for customers and freight is possible. MEGAPARC is considering retail, logistics and mixed-use concepts.

Concept. Parameters are subject to planning, engineering and commercial review.
