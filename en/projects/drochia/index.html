04 · Scenarios

Scenarios for initial evaluation.

The formats MEGAPARC is considering for the site — a starting point for the discussion, not an approved use.

  1. 01

    Retail

    Retail and services for the town and the region.

    To verify: permitted use, access, parking

  2. 02

    Logistics

    Storage and distribution with separate freight access.

    To verify: roads, access loads, utilities

  3. 03

    Mixed format

    Retail facing the road, logistics at the rear — on one site.

    To verify: site configuration, flows, planning regime

These are not approved planning concepts. Final development parameters depend on urban-planning, technical and commercial verification.

Deal structure

Ways to structure the opportunity.

  1. 01

    Sale

    The developer acquires the site.

  2. 02

    Joint development

    MEGAPARC contributes the land or assets and the parties agree the project structure.

  3. 03

    Land against completed real estate

    Part of the land value may be exchanged for completed commercial premises under a separately negotiated structure.

Structures are negotiated project by project and are not automatically available for every site.
