How value is created

How MEGAPARC creates value.

We invest in assets we can acquire, develop, reposition, operate, hold for the long term or exit when it is strategically right.

  1. Acquire

    Commercial buildings — working ones and ones that need a new life — and land for development.

    Drochia Gateway · 2.0 ha · 20,000 m²

  2. Develop

    We build our own projects and renovate and reposition the buildings we own.

    VATRA · Stage: delivery

  3. Operate

    We look after our buildings ourselves: maintenance, tenants, investment in improvements.

    Creangă 78 · Operating property · 100% occupied

  4. Reinvest

    Capital released is put back into existing and new projects. The further strategy for each property is set by its leasing profile, its potential and market conditions.

    Dacia 31 · Development · ≈ 4,800 m²
VATRA · Stage: delivery

What we consider

Which opportunities does MEGAPARC consider?

We consider projects where we can create value through investment, development and subsequent operation. All our current properties are in the Republic of Moldova. We also consider offers from other countries — each on its own merits.

  1. Operating commercial buildings

    Retail, offices, services — buildings that already work or could work better.

  2. Land for development

    Plots in town or at its entrance, with access and clear potential.

  3. Buildings to renovate or reposition

    Old or empty buildings in a good location that deserve a new use.

  4. Clearly structured partnerships

    Taking part in a specific project or developing a property together; terms are discussed individually.
