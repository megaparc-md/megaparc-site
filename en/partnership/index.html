How value is created

Three directions of value creation.

  1. 01

    Real estate investment

    We acquire commercial property and land with development potential.

  2. 02

    Development

    We build new projects, redevelop existing buildings and unlock the potential of property.

  3. 03

    Leasing

    We create commercial space for business and lease it in MEGAPARC properties.

The further strategy for each property is set by its leasing profile, its potential and market conditions.

Capital released is put back into existing and new projects.

What does MEGAPARC consider?

We consider projects where we can create value through investment, development and subsequent operation.

  • Existing commercial real estate
  • Land
  • Redevelopment and reconstruction
  • Joint projects
  • Structured property opportunities

What information is useful?

  • Location
  • Cadastral number
  • Land and building area
  • Photographs
  • Ownership
  • Current use
  • Asking price, if available
  • Planning documents
  • Utilities
  • Occupancy and leases

Not everything is needed at once. The more initial data, the faster we can tell whether it makes sense to continue the discussion.

How does the initial assessment work?

We look at the property from six sides — as at our own future asset.

Economics
Demand, future tenants, costs and timing — worked out before the decision.
Legal
Title to land and building, restrictions, permits.
Technical
Structure, building services, power and condition.
Development
What the property can become: use, area, phases and cost of works.
Leasing
Who will lease, which formats the area needs, how fast the property fills.
Operations
How the building will run for years: upkeep, costs, quality.
