How value is created

Three directions of value creation.

  1. 01

    Real estate investment

    We acquire commercial property and land with development potential.

  2. 02

    Development

    We build new projects, redevelop existing buildings and unlock the potential of property.

  3. 03

    Leasing

    We create commercial space for business and lease it in MEGAPARC properties.

The further strategy for each property is set by its leasing profile, its potential and market conditions.

Capital released is put back into existing and new projects.

What we consider and how we evaluate

Selection criteria and investment due diligence.

What we consider

  • Operating commercial buildings

    Retail, offices, services — buildings that already work or could work better.

  • Buildings to renovate or reposition

    Old or empty buildings in a good location that deserve a new use.

  • Land for development

    Plots in town or at its entrance, with access and clear potential.

  • Projects already started

    Projects with permits or construction under way that need a new owner.

How we evaluate

  • Economics

    Demand, future tenants, costs and timing — worked out before the decision.

  • Legal

    Title to land and building, restrictions, permits.

  • Technical

    Structure, building services, power and condition.

  • Development

    What the property can become: use, area, phases and cost of works.

  • Leasing

    Who will lease, which formats the area needs, how fast the property fills.

  • Operations

    How the building will run for years: upkeep, costs, quality.

Partnership and financing

  • Investors

    Taking part in a specific MEGAPARC project; terms are discussed individually.

  • Banks and financial partners

    Financing the acquisition and development of our own properties.

  • Owners of real estate and land

    Sale or joint development of real estate.
