The chain channel needs sales, price, cost, discounts and retrospective rebates.

For the store, enter the VAT rate, price, customers, average purchase, trading days, cost and rent or staff.

All amounts in euros; the calculation is excl. VAT. Operating contribution — before the company's overheads, depreciation, interest and tax. Chain receivables are shown excl. VAT (conservative).

Cash at the start — CAPEX, preparation, deposit and stock on the opening day; money that arrives later never reduces it. Chain receivables come in as the chains pay, after the payment term, and are part of the monthly cash flow over 120 months; the peak funding need is the lowest point of the cumulative cash flow.

Cost of money — the company's annual cost of capital: a loan rate or the return it requires (no loan is modelled). It never reduces the operating contribution: it shows the cost of tied-up cash (for information only, never subtracted) and discounts the cash flow for the discounted payback. The deposit is returned on termination of the lease, under its terms; the model does not return it within the 120-month horizon.

The Moscova 20 rates, deposit and utilities terms are in the property's commercial terms; the rent calculation is preliminary. The calculator never changes the property's commercial terms.
