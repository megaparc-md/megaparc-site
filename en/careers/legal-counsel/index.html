Legal Counsel

Make every transaction legally sound, protect the company's interests and support its real estate development projects.

The role in focus

Legal certainty for the business

The Legal Counsel provides the company with legal support in both its day-to-day operations and its real estate development projects: advising, developing legal solutions and protecting the company's interests in contracts, in dealings with public authorities and in disputes.

The role is directly involved in the company's legal work, in cooperation with the finance, technical and administrative departments and with management.

What you will do

  1. 01

    Legal support for operations and projects

    • Legal support for the company in its operations and its real estate development projects.
    • Legal advice and legal solutions that protect the company's interests.
    • Legal support in managing the company's properties.
    • Working with the finance, technical, administrative and management functions to resolve legal matters.
  2. 02

    Contracts and legal documentation

    • Reviewing, drafting and negotiating contracts: lease agreements, service agreements, contracts for works, procurement contracts, addenda and others.
    • Preparing and drafting applications, notices, formal demands and other official documents.
    • Keeping the legal documentation for the company's projects and properties recorded and organised.
  3. 03

    Compliance and legal risk

    • Checking that legal documents comply with current legislation.
    • Monitoring legislative changes relevant to the company's business and informing management of their possible implications.
    • Monitoring compliance with contractual obligations and identifying legal risks.
  4. 04

    Representation and disputes

    • Representing the company's interests before public authorities, state institutions, partners and other legal entities or individuals.
    • Preparing official replies to requests from public and private institutions.
    • Taking part in resolving disputes and handling legal situations that may affect the company's interests.

What we are looking for

Essential

  • A completed university degree in Law.
  • Relevant experience in civil law.
  • Experience of drafting, reviewing and negotiating contracts.
  • Advanced Romanian and Russian.

Professional skills

  • Solid computer skills.
  • Excellent communication, organisational and analytical skills.
  • Initiative, a sense of responsibility and attention to detail.
  • Ability to find legal solutions and to meet the deadlines set.
  • Ability to handle several tasks and legal files at the same time.

Nice to have

  • Knowledge of the legislation on real estate, construction and condominium administration.
  • Experience of working with public authorities.
  • Knowledge of English.
  • Category B driving licence and your own car.
