The role in focus

Strategy and company growth

The Chief Executive Officer is accountable for the company as a whole: its strategy, its management team, its real estate portfolio and the delivery of the decisions taken. The role is directly involved in investment decisions and in projects, from concept to completion.

All departments report into this role: construction, technical, real estate and leasing, finance and legal.

What you will do

  1. 01

    Strategy and company leadership

    • Overall coordination of the company and all its departments.
    • Developing and implementing the short-, medium- and long-term development strategy.
    • Setting strategic and operational objectives and monitoring how departments deliver them.
    • Building an effective internal structure and continuously improving the company's processes.
    • Leading, motivating and developing the management team; effective communication between departments and management.
    • Taking strategic decisions on the company's development.
  2. 02

    Developing the company's real estate portfolio

    • Managing the company's portfolio of properties.
    • Strategies to develop the properties, increase their value and improve their returns.
    • Identifying investment opportunities and new projects.
    • Analysing the real estate market and directions for growth.
    • Coordinating leasing, operation and optimisation of space.
    • Developing relationships with tenants, partners and investors.
  3. 03

    Projects and operations

    • Overseeing real estate projects from concept to completion.
    • Coordinating the departments: construction, technical, real estate, finance, legal and other internal functions.
    • Reviewing and approving decisions on budgets, investments and project priorities.
    • Identifying risks and implementing solutions that make the company more efficient.
  4. 04

    Legal and administrative responsibilities

    • Reviewing the company's key contracts and documentation.
    • Ensuring the company operates in line with current legislation.
    • Coordinating relations with public authorities and state institutions.
    • Monitoring that permits, certificates and authorisations are obtained and kept valid.
    • Representing the company with partners, investors and external institutions.

What we are looking for

Essential

  • Relevant experience in management positions — required.
  • Experience of leading several departments and coordinating large teams.
  • Category B driving licence and willingness to use your own car for business travel.

Professional skills

  • Proven leadership and team development skills.
  • Ability to develop and implement business strategies.
  • Experience of setting and monitoring key performance indicators (KPIs).
  • Knowledge of budgeting and financial management.
  • Knowledge of real estate, construction, urban planning and investment.
  • Excellent negotiation, communication and decision-making skills.
  • Strategic thinking, a focus on results and continuous development.

Nice to have

  • Experience of managing companies or investment projects, or of working in real estate or construction.
