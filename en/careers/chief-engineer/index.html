Construction & Engineering

Chief Engineer

Keep the buildings' engineering systems reliable, introduce efficient technical solutions and help the company's properties evolve.

Pay

MDL 30,000

net · during the probation period

Area
Construction & Engineering
Employment
Full-time

The role in focus

Technical reliability, building by building

The Chief Engineer is accountable for the technical condition of the company's buildings, installations and equipment: for their maintenance, for the internal and external engineering networks, for efficient, uninterrupted operation and for compliance with technical regulations.

The role also covers construction, repair, renovation and modernisation works: reviewing designs and preparing terms of reference, cost estimates and budgets, selecting offers, coordinating contractors and accepting completed works. The Chief Engineer leads the technical team and reports regularly to company management.

What you will do

  1. 01

    Technical management and maintenance

    • Full responsibility for the maintenance and technical upkeep of buildings, installations and equipment.
    • Organising the commissioning of new installations, equipment and machinery.
    • Managing and monitoring internal and external engineering networks: electricity, water, sewerage, gas, heating, ventilation, air conditioning and other technical installations.
    • Continuous monitoring of the technical condition of equipment, keeping it running efficiently and without interruption.
    • Organising and coordinating upkeep, maintenance, and routine and major repairs.
    • Identifying where existing buildings, installations and equipment need modernisation, renovation or optimisation.
    • Compliance with current technical rules and regulations, including fire safety and occupational health and safety.
  2. 02

    Designs and technical documentation

    • Reading, interpreting and analysing architectural, construction and engineering-installation designs: architectural plans, sections, elevations, construction details and technical diagrams.
    • Reviewing design documentation and identifying inconsistencies, technical problems or sub-optimal solutions.
    • Preparing technical briefs and terms of reference for construction, renovation, repair, modernisation and fit-out works.
    • Defining the technical, functional and execution requirements for planned projects.
    • Taking part in the analysis and selection of architectural and technical solutions, together with the designers and specialists involved.
    • Checking the technical documentation submitted by designers, contractors and service providers.
    • Taking part in preparing and checking the technical documentation needed to carry out the works.
    • Monitoring that approved designs and technical solutions are followed during execution.
  3. 03

    Coordinating works and managing projects

    • Organising, planning and coordinating construction, repair, renovation and modernisation works.
    • Coordinating technical teams, contractors and service providers.
    • Supervising the works in line with the designs, terms of reference, deadlines and quality requirements.
    • Checking the quality and compliance of the works carried out.
    • Taking part in the acceptance of works and checking the related documents.
    • Identifying and resolving technical problems that arise during execution.
    • Tracking compliance with work schedules and agreed deadlines.
  4. 04

    Cost estimates, budgets, procurement and suppliers

    • Preparing, planning and managing budgets for maintenance, repairs, construction and modernisation.
    • Preparing, analysing and checking cost estimates.
    • Checking quantities of work and estimated costs against the technical documentation.
    • Requesting and analysing commercial and technical offers.
    • Comparing offers and identifying the most efficient solutions in technical and economic terms.
    • Working and negotiating with suppliers, designers, contractors and service providers.
    • Checking the quality of the materials and equipment used in the works.
    • Accepting completed works and checking that they meet the contractual and technical requirements.
  5. 05

    Team management and reporting

    • Organising, coordinating and monitoring the work of the technical team.
    • Setting priorities and allocating tasks according to the company's needs.
    • Identifying technical risks and proposing preventive and corrective measures.
    • Preparing reports on the technical condition of buildings, installations and equipment.
    • Reporting regularly to company management on the works completed, the budgets used and the projects in progress.
    • Proposing solutions to optimise operating, maintenance and administration costs.

What we are looking for

Essential

  • A university degree in electrical power engineering, a technical or engineering field, construction or building installations.
  • At least 5 years of professional experience in a similar position.
  • Practical experience of preparing technical briefs, terms of reference and technical specifications for construction, renovation, repair and modernisation works.
  • Experience of preparing, analysing and checking cost estimates.
  • Category B driving licence and your own car — required.
  • Advanced Romanian and Russian.

Professional skills

  • Sound knowledge of technical standards, the applicable legislation and occupational health and safety requirements.
  • Computer skills: Microsoft Office (Word, Excel), AutoCAD, WinSmeta.
  • Ability to estimate volumes of work, the materials required and the related costs.
  • Ability to coordinate, organise and manage a team.
  • Ability to analyse, identify and resolve technical problems.
  • Negotiation and communication skills in dealing with suppliers, designers and contractors.
  • Good organisation, a sense of responsibility and a focus on results.

Nice to have

  • Experience of managing the maintenance of commercial buildings and engineering installations.
