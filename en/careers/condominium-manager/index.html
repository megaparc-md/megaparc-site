The role in focus

Well-run buildings, disciplined finances

As part of the MEGAPARC team, the Condominium Manager organises, coordinates and runs the condominium and is accountable for the sound operation of the building, its common areas and its installations. Three strands meet in one role: the technical condition of the building, the budget and payments, and relations with owners, residents and tenants.

The role involves organising the administration of the condominium independently and reporting regularly to the company's management on its technical, financial and administrative condition.

What you will do

  1. 01

    Building operation and technical condition

    • Organising, coordinating and administering the condominium's activity.
    • Ensuring the sound functioning and operation of the building, the common areas and the related installations.
    • Continuous monitoring of the technical condition of the building and its engineering systems: water, sewerage, electricity, heating, ventilation and others.
    • Organising and supervising routine maintenance, repairs and technical interventions.
    • Identifying faults and arranging for them to be remedied in good time.
    • Identifying and implementing measures that improve how the building is operated, maintained and administered.
  2. 02

    Suppliers, contracts and service staff

    • Coordinating the work of service staff and service providers.
    • Identifying and selecting suppliers and subcontractors for maintenance and repair works.
    • Negotiating, concluding and monitoring contracts with service and utility providers.
    • Checking the quality of the services provided and compliance with contractual obligations.
  3. 03

    Budget, payments and costs

    • Preparing, planning and managing the condominium's annual administration and maintenance budget; estimating maintenance and administration costs.
    • Monitoring budget execution, managing expenditure and identifying ways to optimise costs.
    • Organising the recording and monitoring of payments for utilities and maintenance costs.
    • Monitoring payments, keeping records of arrears and taking measures to recover debts.
  4. 04

    Relations with owners and residents

    • Managing relations with owners, residents, tenants and their representatives.
    • Handling requests, notifications and complaints from owners and residents.
    • Organising and attending meetings with owners and presenting reports on the condominium's activity.
    • Ensuring compliance with the internal regulations and the rules for using the common areas.
  5. 05

    Documentation, compliance and reporting

    • Keeping and safeguarding the administrative, technical and contractual documentation.
    • Monitoring compliance with occupational health and safety rules, fire safety rules and the applicable legal requirements.
    • Working with public authorities, utility providers and the competent institutions.
    • Preparing periodic reports to the company's management on the technical, financial and administrative condition of the condominium.

What we are looking for

Essential

  • A university degree in business administration, management, economics, law or another relevant field.
  • At least 3 years of professional experience in managing condominiums, buildings or properties.
  • Experience of managing relations with owners, residents, tenants and service providers.
  • Knowledge of Romanian and Russian.

Professional skills

  • Knowledge of the legislation of the Republic of Moldova on condominium administration and the operation of buildings.
  • Knowledge of budget administration, expense records and payment monitoring.
  • Ability to organise and coordinate maintenance and repair works.
  • Negotiation, communication and conflict-resolution skills.
  • Computer skills: Microsoft Word, Microsoft Excel.
  • Responsibility, punctuality, organisational ability and a focus on results.

Nice to have

  • Category B driving licence.
