Commercial Development & Marketing

Real Estate Development, Property Portfolio & Marketing Manager

Unlock the commercial potential of the company's properties, attract strategic tenants and build long-term partnerships.

Pay

MDL 30,000

net · per month · during the probation period

Area
Commercial Development & Marketing
Employment
Full-time

The role in focus

The commercial potential of property

The manager is responsible for putting the company's properties — buildings, commercial space, office space and land plots — to commercial use: attracting tenants and partners, running the leasing process from identifying a prospective tenant to a signed lease, and managing tenant relationships for the full term of each lease.

The role brings together leasing, the management of the company's properties, marketing and market analysis, and contributes directly to occupancy, stable income and the growing value of the property portfolio.

What you will do

  1. 01

    Developing the property portfolio and attracting tenants

    • Actively identifying and attracting new tenants and partners for the available commercial space, office space and land plots.
    • Seeking out prospective tenants, companies, entrepreneurs, investors and commercial partners and developing relationships with them.
    • Actively promoting the available properties and presenting offers to prospective clients; arranging meetings and property presentations.
    • Negotiating commercial terms and concluding lease and cooperation agreements.
    • Giving priority to long-term leases, to keep the company's income stable.
    • Identifying opportunities to attract strategic tenants and developing long-term commercial relationships.
    • Increasing the occupancy of the available space and reducing the periods in which it stands vacant.
    • Identifying opportunities to lease, use and commercialise land plots, as well as investors and partners interested in using or developing the company's properties.
  2. 02

    Managing the company's properties

    • Managing and developing the company's entire property portfolio: buildings, commercial space, office space and land plots.
    • Running the complete leasing process, from identifying prospective tenants to negotiating and signing leases.
    • Maintaining and developing professional relationships with existing tenants.
    • Monitoring compliance with lease terms and managing tenant relationships throughout the term of each lease.
    • Identifying opportunities to renew leases and retaining tenants for the long term.
    • Monitoring incoming payments, managing debtors and working with the finance and legal departments to recover receivables.
    • Tracking the position of each property and finding ways to use it efficiently and to its commercial potential.
    • Identifying opportunities to increase the returns and the value of the property portfolio.
  3. 03

    Property marketing and promotion

    • Developing and implementing marketing and promotion strategies for the properties available for lease or other commercial use.
    • Organising promotion campaigns to attract new tenants, investors and partners.
    • Promoting commercial space, office space and land plots through appropriate advertising channels, and identifying the most effective channels for reaching prospective tenants and partners.
    • Publishing and updating property offers on the company website, on specialist platforms and in other promotion channels.
    • Managing promotion on social media and other online communication channels.
    • Preparing presentation and promotional materials for the properties.
    • Monitoring the effectiveness of marketing campaigns, analysing the results of promotion, refining the strategies and proposing improvements.
    • Ensuring a professional and attractive image of the company online.
  4. 04

    Analysis, development and reporting

    • Analysing the real estate market, the competition, rent levels and market trends.
    • Identifying opportunities to develop the property portfolio and increase its value.
    • Analysing the commercial and economic potential of the land and properties in the company's portfolio.
    • Preparing proposals to improve the returns on the properties and presenting them to the company's management.
    • Monitoring performance indicators and preparing regular reports for management.
    • Working effectively with the legal, finance and technical departments and with other internal functions.

What we are looking for

Essential

  • A university degree in business administration, economics, management, marketing or another relevant field.
  • At least 5 years' professional experience in real estate portfolio management, marketing, commercial leasing, sales, business development or property management.
  • Hands-on experience of leasing commercial and/or office space.
  • Experience of identifying and attracting prospective tenants and clients, negotiating with them and developing long-term commercial relationships.
  • Experience of managing contractual relationships with tenants and building long-term relationships.
  • Experience of monitoring incoming payments, managing debtors and recovering receivables.
  • Experience of promoting, leasing or commercialising real estate, including land plots.
  • Category B driving licence and availability to travel.

Professional skills

  • Solid knowledge of marketing, advertising and promotion, including digital marketing.
  • Ability to develop and implement marketing strategies for promoting and leasing real estate.
  • Knowledge of the real estate market: competitor analysis, rent levels, occupancy and market trends.
  • Ability to identify opportunities to develop the property portfolio and increase its value.
  • Knowledge of online promotion, real estate platforms and social media.
  • Advanced Microsoft Office skills (Excel, Word, PowerPoint).
  • A focus on results and on meeting the agreed objectives; a commercial and entrepreneurial mindset.
  • Excellent negotiation and persuasion skills; marketing, promotion and sales skills; the ability to find and win new clients.
  • Strategic thinking and a focus on development; analytical and decision-making ability.
  • Organisation and planning skills and the ability to manage several projects at once.
  • Initiative, a proactive approach and autonomy at work; responsibility, integrity and attention to detail.
  • Excellent communication skills and the ability to work with tenants, partners and internal departments.

Nice to have

  • Experience of managing online content and promoting property offers.
  • Experience of using CRM systems.

How performance is measured

Performance is assessed against a set of indicators, including:

  1. 01

    Occupancy and commercial use of the available space and land.

  2. 02

    Number of new tenants attracted and leases concluded.

  3. 03

    Number of long-term leases concluded.

  4. 04

    Income generated from leasing and operating the properties.

  5. 05

    Growth in occupancy and shorter vacancy periods.

  6. 06

    Lower receivables and effective management of debtors.

  7. 07

    Retention and renewal of contractual relationships with existing tenants.

  8. 08

    Number of new partners and investors attracted.

  9. 09

    Effectiveness of marketing and promotion campaigns.

  10. 10

    Development and improvement of the company's online promotion channels.

  11. 11

    Greater visibility and appeal of the entire property portfolio.
