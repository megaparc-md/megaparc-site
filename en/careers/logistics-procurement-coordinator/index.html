Logistics, Facilities, Transport & Procurement Coordinator

Keep the company running smoothly day to day, find efficient solutions and make careful, economical use of its resources.

Pay

MDL 20,000

net · during the probation period

Area
Operations & Facilities

The role in focus

Resources, efficiently organised

The Coordinator keeps the company's logistics, facilities, transport and supply activities running smoothly: managing the company's day-to-day needs, organising purchasing and finding the most advantageous offers on the market, looking after the goods and premises in the role's care, and maintaining and monitoring the company's vehicle fleet.

The role includes driving the company's Administrator (the head of the company) and carrying out the organisational tasks the Administrator assigns — which is why we are looking for a responsible, organised, practical and trustworthy person.

What you will do

  1. 01

    Logistics and procurement

    • Identifying, consolidating and managing the company's needs for goods, materials, equipment, consumables and other items required for day-to-day operations.
    • Continuous, in-depth study of the market to find products, suppliers and advantageous offers; building relationships with new suppliers and maintaining those with existing ones.
    • Requesting quotations from several suppliers, then analysing and comparing them on price, product quality, delivery conditions and payment terms.
    • Negotiating commercial terms and identifying the most advantageous purchasing solutions, with the best balance of quality and price.
    • Organising and making purchases on the basis of identified and approved needs; tracking orders and deliveries.
    • Receiving goods: checking the quantity and quality of what is delivered against the orders.
    • Monitoring stock levels and preventing shortages of the materials and goods needed.
    • Keeping purchasing records and reporting on offers, costs and expenditure; making efficient, economical use of the budgets allocated for purchasing.
  2. 02

    Facilities and company property

    • Organising, managing and supervising facilities and housekeeping activities.
    • Keeping the premises, equipment and goods in the role's care well maintained and in working order; sound stewardship of the properties and goods entrusted to the role.
    • Maintaining order, cleanliness and proper conditions in the premises looked after.
    • Identifying maintenance and repair needs; organising and monitoring these works.
    • Coordinating service providers and supervising the quality of the work carried out.
    • Managing housekeeping needs and stocks of materials and consumables.
    • Overseeing the proper and economical use of company property.
    • Identifying facilities problems and acting promptly to resolve them.
  3. 03

    Transport and the company's vehicle fleet

    • Driving the company's Administrator (the head of the company) safely, comfortably and punctually; planning and organising the Administrator's journeys according to the agreed schedule.
    • Strict compliance with traffic rules and with safety standards for passenger transport.
    • Managing and monitoring the technical condition and appearance of all company vehicles; keeping the cars clean, in good working order and presentable.
    • Ensuring periodic servicing and mandatory technical inspections are carried out on time.
    • Monitoring validity dates and arranging the timely renewal of compulsory RCA motor insurance and, where applicable, CASCO; tracking the expiry dates of all vehicle documents.
    • Organising and monitoring vehicle maintenance and repairs; booking cars in for service and coordinating the necessary technical work.
    • Monitoring fuel consumption and vehicle running costs; ensuring company vehicles are used correctly, responsibly and economically.
    • Keeping a record for each vehicle — servicing, insurance, repairs, technical inspections and other obligations; informing the Administrator in good time of faults, repair needs and fleet-related costs.
  4. 04

    Administrative and organisational responsibilities

    • Carrying out organisational and administrative tasks assigned by the Administrator.
    • Managing daily tasks efficiently and setting priorities.
    • Monitoring deadlines and completing the tasks taken on in time.
    • Reporting needs, problems identified and proposed solutions.
    • Regular reporting on purchasing, facilities expenditure and the state of the vehicle fleet.
    • Confidentiality and discretion in carrying out the role.

What we are looking for

Essential

  • At least 5 years of professional experience in a similar position.
  • Category B driving licence — required.

Professional skills

  • Sound knowledge of road safety rules and of safety practice in passenger transport.
  • Good knowledge of the city and a good sense of direction.
  • Ability to study the market, identify suppliers, and analyse and compare commercial offers.
  • Negotiation skills and a drive to obtain the best purchasing conditions; ability to assess value for money and identify cost-effective solutions.
  • Ability to manage and monitor a vehicle fleet, including records of servicing, insurance, repairs and the related documents.
  • Practical knowledge of vehicle maintenance and operation.
  • Ability to plan and track mandatory deadlines so that servicing, inspections and insurance are carried out or renewed on time.
  • Practical skills in maintaining and supervising the premises and goods in the role's care and keeping them in working order.
  • Very good organisation and planning skills and the ability to handle several tasks at once.
  • A practical mindset, initiative and the ability to identify and solve problems.
  • A responsible attitude towards the company's goods, properties and resources.
  • Reliability, punctuality, discretion and professional conduct; a neat appearance, a respectful attitude and the ability to keep information confidential.

Nice to have

  • Experience in purchasing, supply, logistics or supplier relations — an important advantage.
