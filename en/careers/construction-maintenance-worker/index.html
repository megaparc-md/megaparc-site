Construction & Maintenance Worker

Put your craft to work on real buildings. Your professionalism ensures their quality, reliability and durability.

The role in focus

Craft you can see

The Construction & Maintenance Worker carries out maintenance, repair and finishing work in the company's buildings and premises — from routine upkeep and fixing defects to the renovation and refitting of interior and exterior spaces.

The work takes place at the company's different locations, independently and in a team, with attention to the quality of the work done and to deadlines.

What you will do

  1. 01

    Maintenance and repairs

    • Routine maintenance and repair work in buildings.
    • General upkeep of buildings: doors, windows, roofs, finishes and more.
    • Identifying and fixing defects in buildings and premises.
  2. 02

    Construction, finishing and renovation

    • Masonry, plastering, floor and wall tiling, painting and decorating, and other finishing work.
    • Taking part in the renovation and refitting of interior and exterior spaces.
    • General construction work, as the company's needs require.
  3. 03

    Tools, safety and rules

    • Proper use of work tools and equipment.
    • Following occupational health and safety rules.
    • Following the company's internal rules.

What we are looking for

Essential

  • At least 5 years of experience in construction or building maintenance.
  • Readiness for physical work and for travel to the company's different locations.

Professional skills

  • Practical knowledge of construction, repair and finishing work.
  • Reliability, a sense of responsibility and a willingness to work.
  • Ability to work both independently and in a team.
  • Attention to the quality of the work done and to meeting the deadlines set.
